Logo usage rules

Simple rules to keep our brand consistent everywhere it appears.

Do

  • Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of one letter of the name.
  • Keep the original proportions when resizing.
  • Keep the logo at least 24 px tall on screens and 10 mm tall in print.
  • Place it on backgrounds with strong contrast.
  • Write the name as "Wind Developers" in text.

Don't

  • Stretch, squash, rotate or skew the logo.
  • Change its colors or add effects such as shadows, glows or outlines.
  • Place it on busy images or low-contrast backgrounds.
  • Redraw it, crop it or add elements to it.
  • Suggest endorsement or partnership by Wind Developers without written permission.
